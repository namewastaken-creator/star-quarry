Install it

Read your device's steps before you open the game. The game opens in a new tab and this page stays open in the old one, so you can switch back to it at any time.

  • Android
  • iPhone & iPad
  • PC & Mac

The other devices are greyed out because the game installs on the device you're reading this on. To install it on another phone, tablet or computer, open this page on that device.

Android

Your deviceChrome
  1. Open the game only in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu at the top right, then Add to Home screen (or Install app), then Install.
  3. Close the Chrome tab and open Star Quarry from its new icon on your home screen.
Open the game ↗namewastaken-creator.github.io/star-quarry

From the icon it runs full screen and works offline.

iPhone & iPad

Your deviceSafari
  1. Open the game only in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow pointing up). On iPhone it's at the bottom of the screen; on iPad, at the top.
  3. Scroll down, tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  4. Close the Safari tab and open Star Quarry from its new icon on your home screen.
Open the game ↗namewastaken-creator.github.io/star-quarry

Always play from the home-screen icon. It keeps its own save, separate from Safari, so a game started in a Safari tab won't carry over.

PC & Mac

Your deviceChrome or Edge
  1. Open the game only in Chrome or Microsoft Edge.
  2. Chrome: click the install icon at the right end of the address bar (a small screen with a down arrow), or go to ⋮ › Cast, save and share › Install page as app.
    Edge: go to ⋯ › Apps › Install this site as an app.
  3. Star Quarry opens in its own window, with a Start menu or desktop shortcut. You can close the browser tab.
Open the game ↗namewastaken-creator.github.io/star-quarry

Good to know

  • Updates install themselves the next time you open the game, and your progress carries over.
  • Your save stays on that device. Progress doesn't move between your phone and computer, and clearing your browser's site data erases it.
  • New to the game? Open the Warp tab and tap Open the user guide to see how everything works, from mining crews to warps.
  • Tips and tricks is the last section of the user guide. Read it once you've got going.
  • The version you're playing is shown on the Warp tab.
